# Say It with Flowers Copy

## Brief

Audience: people interested in florist. Context: a warm seasonal email audience. Primary goal: choose their valentine’s bouquet. Offer: Valentine’s bouquets from £38. Tone follows the fictional sender and campaign; all commercial claims are illustrative and need owner verification. No testimonials, statistics or real-world endorsements are invented.

## Selected copy

Subject: Valentine’s flowers, with your words attached

Preheader: Seasonal stems, thoughtful wrapping and a note from you.

Headline: Say it in your own way.

Introduction: A little wild, a little unexpected and made to feel personal. Choose a Valentine’s bouquet with a message only you could write.

Primary CTA: Choose their Valentine’s bouquet

Offer: Valentine’s bouquets from £38

Practical detail: Add your personal message before checkout.

Section: Flowers with a little personality.

### The soft-spoken one

Pale seasonal blooms with an easy, gathered-from-the-garden feel.

### The bold romantic

Deep reds and expressive stems that take up a little more room.

Closing headline: The flowers are ours. The words are yours.

## Rationale

The subject identifies the actual seasonal opportunity. The headline gives Stemstory its own voice; the introduction explains the offer in everyday language. Each section answers a concrete question about choosing, attending or using it. Both action buttons share the same specific destination. Add your personal message before checkout. supplies a practical decision point rather than unsupported proof or artificial urgency.

## Unused alternatives

Headline B: Valentine’s flowers, with your words attached — A more literal seasonal message.

Headline C: The flowers are ours. The words are yours. — A warmer benefit-led opening for an already familiar audience.

CTA B: Find their Valentine’s bouquet — An alternative action verb with the same destination.

CTA C: Choose their Valentine’s bouquet with Stemstory — A sender-specific version for a less familiar audience.
