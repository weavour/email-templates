# Good Furniture for Less Copy

## Brief

Audience: people interested in furniture and homewares. Context: a warm seasonal email audience. Primary goal: explore the black friday edit. Offer: 20% off selected furniture. Tone follows the fictional sender and campaign; all commercial claims are illustrative and need owner verification. No testimonials, statistics or real-world endorsements are invented.

## Selected copy

Subject: Black Friday, considered: selected furniture, 20% off

Preheader: A smaller edit of pieces worth living with.

Headline: Good furniture. A better moment to bring it home.

Introduction: Our Black Friday selection is here: considered pieces for the rooms you use every day, with 20% off selected designs.

Primary CTA: Explore the Black Friday edit

Offer: 20% off selected furniture

Practical detail: The discount is shown on eligible product pages.

Section: Start with the pieces that stay.

### The Oak Lounge Chair

A solid frame, a generous seat and room to put your feet up.

### The Everyday Side Table

A quiet companion for the book, the lamp and the last cup of tea.

Closing headline: Buy for the room you want to live in.

## Rationale

The subject identifies the actual seasonal opportunity. The headline gives Formroom its own voice; the introduction explains the offer in everyday language. Each section answers a concrete question about choosing, attending or using it. Both action buttons share the same specific destination. The discount is shown on eligible product pages. supplies a practical decision point rather than unsupported proof or artificial urgency.
