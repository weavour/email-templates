# A Thoughtful Thank You Copy

## Brief

Audience: people interested in corporate gifting. Context: a warm seasonal email audience. Primary goal: build my christmas gift plan. Offer: Client and team gifting, from 20 boxes. Tone follows the fictional sender and campaign; all commercial claims are illustrative and need owner verification. No testimonials, statistics or real-world endorsements are invented.

## Selected copy

Subject: Christmas gifts for the people behind your year

Preheader: Thoughtful client and team gifting, with the logistics handled.

Headline: Thank them like you mean it.

Introduction: Send a Christmas gift that feels considered. We’ll help you choose the contents, add your message and organise the deliveries.

Primary CTA: Build my Christmas gift plan

Offer: Client and team gifting, from 20 boxes

Practical detail: Request a quote for your budget and delivery list.

Section: A good gift. A simpler process.

### Choose

Tell us who it’s for, what you’d like to spend and where it needs to go.

### Personalise

Add your brand quietly and write a message in your own voice.

### Send

Approve the final box and delivery plan before anything leaves the studio.

Closing headline: Your thanks. Beautifully delivered.

## Rationale

The subject identifies the actual seasonal opportunity. The headline gives Parcel & Co. its own voice; the introduction explains the offer in everyday language. Each section answers a concrete question about choosing, attending or using it. Both action buttons share the same specific destination. Request a quote for your budget and delivery list. supplies a practical decision point rather than unsupported proof or artificial urgency.

## Unused alternatives

Headline B: Christmas gifts for the people behind your year — A more literal seasonal message.

Headline C: Your thanks. Beautifully delivered. — A warmer benefit-led opening for an already familiar audience.

CTA B: Plan my Christmas gift plan — An alternative action verb with the same destination.

CTA C: Build my Christmas gift plan with Parcel & Co. — A sender-specific version for a less familiar audience.
