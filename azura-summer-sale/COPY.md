# A Different Shade of Blue Copy

## Brief

Audience: people interested in coastal hotel. Context: a warm seasonal email audience. Primary goal: find my summer stay. Offer: 15% off selected midweek stays. Tone follows the fictional sender and campaign; all commercial claims are illustrative and need owner verification. No testimonials, statistics or real-world endorsements are invented.

## Selected copy

Subject: Your midweek summer stay, with 15% off selected rooms

Preheader: A quieter coastal escape, with breakfast included.

Headline: A few days in a different shade of blue.

Introduction: Our summer stay offer gives you 15% off selected midweek rooms. Pick your dates and leave a little time for the pool.

Primary CTA: Find my summer stay

Offer: 15% off selected midweek stays

Practical detail: Breakfast included on eligible bookings.

Section: Take the day as it comes.

### Wake slowly

Start with breakfast before you decide where to go.

### Follow the water

Stay by the pool or ask us about the local coast.

### Keep one afternoon free

A book, a shaded chair and no next appointment.

Closing headline: Book the room. Leave space for the rest.

## Rationale

The subject identifies the actual seasonal opportunity. The headline gives Azura Rooms its own voice; the introduction explains the offer in everyday language. Each section answers a concrete question about choosing, attending or using it. Both action buttons share the same specific destination. Breakfast included on eligible bookings. supplies a practical decision point rather than unsupported proof or artificial urgency.
