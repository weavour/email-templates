# The Winter Wardrobe Copy

## Brief

Audience: people interested in contemporary clothing. Context: a warm seasonal email audience. Primary goal: shop the black friday wardrobe. Offer: 25% off selected styles. Tone follows the fictional sender and campaign; all commercial claims are illustrative and need owner verification. No testimonials, statistics or real-world endorsements are invented.

## Selected copy

Subject: The Black Friday wardrobe edit: 25% off selected pieces

Preheader: Quiet staples, useful layers and fewer wardrobe questions.

Headline: Wear it now. Reach for it next year.

Introduction: Build around the pieces you’ll keep reaching for. Our Black Friday edit takes 25% off selected coats, knits and everyday layers.

Primary CTA: Shop the Black Friday wardrobe

Offer: 25% off selected styles

Practical detail: Check eligible colours and sizes on each product page.

Section: A wardrobe that works together.

### The long coat

An easy outer layer for workdays, weekends and everything between.

### The soft knit

A simple shape that sits just as well over a shirt as under a coat.

Closing headline: Fewer pieces. More ways to wear them.

## Rationale

The subject identifies the actual seasonal opportunity. The headline gives Threadline its own voice; the introduction explains the offer in everyday language. Each section answers a concrete question about choosing, attending or using it. Both action buttons share the same specific destination. Check eligible colours and sizes on each product page. supplies a practical decision point rather than unsupported proof or artificial urgency.

Only the selected copy ships in the email. No SEO metadata is required for this artifact. Use verified product materials, stock, sizing, pricing and promotion terms before sending.
