# Your First Social Mile Copy

## Brief

Audience: people interested in community running club. Context: a warm seasonal email audience. Primary goal: join the new year run-walk. Offer: First group session · Free. Tone follows the fictional sender and campaign; all commercial claims are illustrative and need owner verification. No testimonials, statistics or real-world endorsements are invented.

## Selected copy

Subject: A friendly first run for the New Year

Preheader: Run, walk or mix the two. Come meet the group.

Headline: Your first mile can be a social one.

Introduction: Start the year with a welcoming run-walk group. We’ll meet you at your pace, introduce you to the route and finish with a coffee stop.

Primary CTA: Join the New Year run-walk

Offer: First group session · Free

Practical detail: An easy local route with run-walk options.

Section: A simple way to start moving together.

### Meet

Find the group leader at the park entrance.

### Move

Choose a run-walk pace that feels comfortable for you.

### Stay for coffee

Get to know a few names before the next session.

Closing headline: A new year. A few new running friends.

## Rationale

The subject identifies the actual seasonal opportunity. The headline gives Miles Ahead its own voice; the introduction explains the offer in everyday language. Each section answers a concrete question about choosing, attending or using it. Both action buttons share the same specific destination. An easy local route with run-walk options. supplies a practical decision point rather than unsupported proof or artificial urgency.
