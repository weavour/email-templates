# Black Friday copy — Wayfarer

## Brief

Audience: people interested in guided travel experiences. Context: a warm seasonal email audience. Primary goal: find my next escape. Offer: £100 off selected departures. Tone follows the fictional sender and campaign; all commercial claims are illustrative and need owner verification. No testimonials, statistics or real-world endorsements are invented.

## Selected copy

Subject: £100 off selected Wayfarer escapes this Black Friday

Preheader: Small groups, thoughtful routes and somewhere new to go.

Headline: A good story starts with a change of scene.

Introduction: Our Black Friday travel edit brings selected small-group escapes into reach, with £100 off eligible departures.

Primary CTA: Find my next escape

Offer: £100 off selected departures

Practical detail: Choose an eligible trip and date on the booking page.

Section: Pick the pace that suits you.

### A weekend away

A short change of scene with the route already considered.

### A longer wander

More time for the places between the obvious stops.

Closing headline: Bring your curiosity. We’ll bring the route.

## Rationale

The subject identifies the actual seasonal opportunity. The headline gives Wayfarer its own voice; the introduction explains the offer in everyday language. Each section answers a concrete question about choosing, attending or using it. Both action buttons share the same specific destination. Choose an eligible trip and date on the booking page. supplies a practical decision point rather than unsupported proof or artificial urgency.
