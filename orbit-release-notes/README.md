# A little more room to focus

A typography-led product update for **Orbit**, a fictional collaboration application. This original design takes inspiration from the breadth of campaign types in [Unlayer’s template gallery](https://unlayer.com/templates), without copying a template or its artwork.

## Design

A compact sender/date masthead leads to a split release plate: an oversized cobalt **3.2** faces the benefit statement. A full-width cobalt decision-thread story anchors the email. Alternating two-column feature rows use different proportions and pale blue/white surfaces, followed by a shorter changelog and an ink-colored invitation to return to the application. The composition is intentionally text-led rather than an imitation product screenshot.

Palette: cobalt `#1748d1`, pale blue `#edf3ff`, ink `#14213b`, muted blue `#445574`, white `#ffffff`, outer `#e5ebf4`. Arial and system sans-serif fallbacks preserve the intended email typography. Native editable headings, paragraphs, sections, columns, spacers, dividers, buttons and the unsubscribe block make up the entire message. There is no HTML block or text baked into an image.

## Image provenance

No images. The release plate and feature composition are native editable text and layout blocks.

## Personalization and sending

Orbit, release 3.2, feature descriptions and the October 2026 release date are fictional example content. Replace the sender, product features, release number, date and `https://example.com/orbit/...` destinations with your real campaign information. Update the footer with your sender details. Keep `{{unsubscribe_url}}` intact until your delivery system substitutes the recipient’s unsubscribe destination. Weavour exports the document; your sending system handles delivery.

The JSON is the editable source. Matching HTML and 800px/375px screenshots are generated through the private application’s actual Weavour renderer before inclusion in the catalog. Browser previews do not replace testing in the email clients your recipients use.
