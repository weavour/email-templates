# His First Good Cup Copy

## Brief

Audience: people interested in specialty coffee. Context: a warm seasonal email audience. Primary goal: choose his coffee gift. Offer: Father’s Day coffee gifts from £22. Tone follows the fictional sender and campaign; all commercial claims are illustrative and need owner verification. No testimonials, statistics or real-world endorsements are invented.

## Selected copy

Subject: A Father’s Day gift for the first cup

Preheader: Fresh coffee and useful brewing extras, ready to give.

Headline: For his first good cup of the day.

Introduction: A fresh roast, a favourite mug and a quiet five minutes. Find a Father’s Day coffee gift that fits his morning.

Primary CTA: Choose his coffee gift

Offer: Father’s Day coffee gifts from £22

Practical detail: Choose whole bean or ground for his brewer.

Section: A better fit for his coffee ritual.

### The morning duo

Two seasonal roasts for a little variety in the cupboard.

### The brewing companion

A simple ceramic cup and a bag of his next favourite roast.

Closing headline: Start with something he’ll use.

## Rationale

The subject identifies the actual seasonal opportunity. The headline gives First Light Roasters its own voice; the introduction explains the offer in everyday language. Each section answers a concrete question about choosing, attending or using it. Both action buttons share the same specific destination. Choose whole bean or ground for his brewer. supplies a practical decision point rather than unsupported proof or artificial urgency.
