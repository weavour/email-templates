Audience: people interested in stationery. Context: a warm seasonal email audience. Primary goal: shop the school-stationery edit. Offer: Back-to-school essentials from £4. ## Rationale

The subject identifies the actual seasonal opportunity. The headline gives Paperpath its own voice; the introduction explains the offer in everyday language. Each section answers a concrete question about choosing, attending or using it. Both action buttons share the same specific destination. Build your own set from the new collection. supplies a practical decision point rather than unsupported proof or artificial urgency.
