# Fireside Christmas Copy

## Brief

Audience: people interested in alpine boutique hotel. Context: a warm seasonal email audience. Primary goal: plan my christmas stay. Offer: Three nights from £495 for two. Tone follows the fictional sender and campaign; all commercial claims are illustrative and need owner verification. No testimonials, statistics or real-world endorsements are invented.

## Selected copy

Subject: Your Christmas escape, by the fire

Preheader: Three nights, breakfast and a quieter kind of Christmas.

Headline: Christmas, with room to slow down.

Introduction: Trade the full calendar for a warm fire and a view of the pines. Our Christmas stay gives you three nights to settle in.

Primary CTA: Plan my Christmas stay

Offer: Three nights from £495 for two

Practical detail: Breakfast and a fireside welcome included.

Section: Make space for the good part.

### Arrive

Leave your bags, warm your hands and take the rest of the day as it comes.

### Wake slowly

Breakfast waits downstairs. The snowy trail starts just beyond the door.

### Stay awhile

Choose your room and Christmas dates before you book.

Closing headline: A little less rush. A little more Christmas.

## Rationale

The subject identifies the actual seasonal opportunity. The headline gives Winterhearth its own voice; the introduction explains the offer in everyday language. Each section answers a concrete question about choosing, attending or using it. Both action buttons share the same specific destination. Breakfast and a fireside welcome included. supplies a practical decision point rather than unsupported proof or artificial urgency.

## Unused alternatives

Headline B: Your Christmas escape, by the fire — A more literal seasonal message.

Headline C: A little less rush. A little more Christmas. — A warmer benefit-led opening for an already familiar audience.

CTA B: Arrange my Christmas stay — An alternative action verb with the same destination.

CTA C: Plan my Christmas stay with Winterhearth — A sender-specific version for a less familiar audience.
