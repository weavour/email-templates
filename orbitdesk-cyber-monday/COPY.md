# Cyber Monday copy — Orbitdesk

## Brief

Audience: people interested in team productivity software. Context: a warm seasonal email audience. Primary goal: see the cyber monday plan. Offer: 20% off your first annual plan. Tone follows the fictional sender and campaign; all commercial claims are illustrative and need owner verification. No testimonials, statistics or real-world endorsements are invented.

## Selected copy

Subject: Cyber Monday: 20% off your first Orbitdesk annual plan

Preheader: A clearer place for the work your team shares.

Headline: Less chasing. More doing.

Introduction: Give your team one place for projects, notes and the next clear step. Our Cyber Monday offer takes 20% off your first annual plan.

Primary CTA: See the Cyber Monday plan

Offer: 20% off your first annual plan

Practical detail: For eligible new annual subscriptions.

Section: Keep the work close to the conversation.

### Projects

Put owners, due dates and next steps where the whole team can find them.

### Notes

Keep the useful context beside the work it explains.

### A clear start

Compare the plan limits and set up a workspace for your team.

Closing headline: Make the next step easier to see.

## Rationale

The subject identifies the actual seasonal opportunity. The headline gives Orbitdesk its own voice; the introduction explains the offer in everyday language. Each section answers a concrete question about choosing, attending or using it. Both action buttons share the same specific destination. For eligible new annual subscriptions. supplies a practical decision point rather than unsupported proof or artificial urgency.
