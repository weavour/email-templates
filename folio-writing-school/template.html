Dear writer,

 

You don’t need a finished idea to begin. A conversation overheard. A room you remember. One detail that refuses to leave. That’s enough.

 

Our small-group writing studio gives those beginnings a place to grow: six weeks of practical exercises, thoughtful feedback and time set aside for your own work.

 

Come as you are. Bring a notebook.

 

Mara Ellis · Course host
