# A place for your first sentence

A fictional Folio Writing School course invitation built from native editable Weavour headings, paragraphs, sections, responsive columns, a divider, button and unsubscribe block.

## Design

A literary correspondence layout: the Folio masthead opens directly into a personal letter; a pale-yellow exercise leaf interrupts the letter with an original writing prompt; a paired Begin / Build syllabus leads into practical booking details. The burgundy closing colophon gives the email a deliberate ending.

Palette: burgundy #542733, paper #fbf7ee, yellow #f3e8ad, muted ink #70535b. Georgia carries the literary headings and excerpt; Arial keeps course information clear. Body copy is 16px, the closing unsubscribe is 14px, and the main title is 42px. Native two-column content remains paired on mobile, keeping the Begin and Build syllabus side by side.

## Content and personalization

Folio, Mara Ellis, the course, schedule, price and capacity are fictional illustrative campaign content. Dates run on Tuesdays from 12 January through 16 February 2027. The first-sentence exercise and all other copy were written for this template; no quotations from real authors are used.

Replace brand, host, price, capacity, timing, sender details and example.com booking destination before sending. Preserve and substitute {{unsubscribe_url}} in your delivery system. Weavour hosts and exports the design; your sending system handles delivery.

Intentionally text-led. No image assets, generated artwork, remote fonts or image-hosting dependency. All visible text remains editable. Root catalog tooling generates matching HTML and desktop/mobile screenshots using the actual editor exporter.
