# A Little More Life Copy

## Brief

Audience: people interested in community repair café. Context: a warm seasonal email audience. Primary goal: reserve a repair-café slot. Offer: Earth Day repair café · Free entry. Tone follows the fictional sender and campaign; all commercial claims are illustrative and need owner verification. No testimonials, statistics or real-world endorsements are invented.

## Selected copy

Subject: Earth Day: bring something worth mending

Preheader: A community repair café, with a place at the table for you.

Headline: Before you replace it, bring it.

Introduction: Mark Earth Day with a practical kind of care. Our repair café brings neighbours and useful skills around one table to see what can be mended.

Primary CTA: Reserve a repair-café slot

Offer: Earth Day repair café · Free entry

Practical detail: Reserve a slot and tell us what you’re bringing.

Section: Help us help your favourite things.

### Describe it

Tell us what the item is and what seems to be wrong.

### Bring the pieces

Include parts, fastenings and any useful information you have.

### Work together

Meet a volunteer and explore whether a repair is possible.

Closing headline: A little skill. A little more life.

## Rationale

The subject identifies the actual seasonal opportunity. The headline gives Mend Together its own voice; the introduction explains the offer in everyday language. Each section answers a concrete question about choosing, attending or using it. Both action buttons share the same specific destination. Reserve a slot and tell us what you’re bringing. supplies a practical decision point rather than unsupported proof or artificial urgency.
