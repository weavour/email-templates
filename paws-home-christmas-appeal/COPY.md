# A Warm Bed for Christmas Copy

## Brief

Audience: people interested in animal rescue. Context: a warm seasonal email audience. Primary goal: give a warmer christmas. Offer: Choose a one-off Christmas gift. Tone follows the fictional sender and campaign; all commercial claims are illustrative and need owner verification. No testimonials, statistics or real-world endorsements are invented.

## Selected copy

Subject: Help make the shelter warmer this Christmas

Preheader: Support the food, bedding and care animals need while they wait.

Headline: A warm bed for the holidays.

Introduction: While we look for their next family, help us make the shelter feel a little more like home this Christmas.

Primary CTA: Give a warmer Christmas

Offer: Choose a one-off Christmas gift

Practical detail: You can choose an amount that suits you.

Section: Small comforts. Everyday care.

### Food

Help stock the meals animals need while they stay with us.

### Warmth

Support clean bedding, blankets and comfortable places to rest.

### Care

Contribute to the practical costs of a safe, welcoming shelter.

Closing headline: Until they find a home, help us make one here.

## Rationale

The subject identifies the actual seasonal opportunity. The headline gives Paws & Home its own voice; the introduction explains the offer in everyday language. Each section answers a concrete question about choosing, attending or using it. Both action buttons share the same specific destination. You can choose an amount that suits you. supplies a practical decision point rather than unsupported proof or artificial urgency.

## Unused alternatives

Headline B: Help make the shelter warmer this Christmas — A more literal seasonal message.

Headline C: Until they find a home, help us make one here. — A warmer benefit-led opening for an already familiar audience.

CTA B: Support a warmer Christmas — An alternative action verb with the same destination.

CTA C: Give a warmer Christmas with Paws & Home — A sender-specific version for a less familiar audience.

Only the selected copy ships in the email. No SEO metadata is required for this artifact. Donation examples describe the fictional campaign’s work, not guaranteed allocations.
