# A Clearer Start Copy

## Brief

Audience: people interested in small-business bookkeeping. Context: a warm seasonal email audience. Primary goal: book my new year bookkeeping review. Offer: New Year bookkeeping review · £95. Tone follows the fictional sender and campaign; all commercial claims are illustrative and need owner verification. No testimonials, statistics or real-world endorsements are invented.

## Selected copy

Subject: Start the year with a clearer bookkeeping plan

Preheader: A focused review for small-business owners.

Headline: A clearer start for your books.

Introduction: Begin the year knowing what’s organised and what still needs attention. Our bookkeeping review turns a loose pile of tasks into a practical next-step list.

Primary CTA: Book my New Year bookkeeping review

Offer: New Year bookkeeping review · £95

Practical detail: A focused review and a written action list.

Section: Know what happens next.

### Bring the basics

We’ll tell you which records to prepare before the call.

### Review together

Walk through your current bookkeeping process and open questions.

### Leave with a list

Receive a practical summary of the agreed next steps.

Closing headline: Less wondering where to start.

## Rationale

The subject identifies the actual seasonal opportunity. The headline gives Clearbooks Studio its own voice; the introduction explains the offer in everyday language. Each section answers a concrete question about choosing, attending or using it. Both action buttons share the same specific destination. A focused review and a written action list. supplies a practical decision point rather than unsupported proof or artificial urgency.
