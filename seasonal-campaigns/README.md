# Forty seasonal email designs

Forty original native editable emails using frontend-design and copywriting, across thirteen marketing occasions and eleven catalog industries. Each sender has its own art direction, offer and voice. Thirteen composition types include travel postcards, menus, fashion lookbooks, admission tickets, letters, offer posters, field guides, programmes, ritual cards, catalogues, service checklists, impact appeals and product bulletins.

Each design uses its own newly generated continuous background on a native outer layout; transparent nested content keeps the surface continuous as text reflows. Typography, descriptions, offers and actions remain live editable blocks. Image subjects, spacing, type hierarchy, reading order and campaign arguments vary across the collection.

## Files and personalization

Every template directory has native JSON, the actual Weavour HTML export, an offline local HTML preview, desktop/mobile screenshots, one original background image with an immutable filename, its exact generation prompt, and copy rationale with unused headline/CTA alternatives. The [collection inventory](collection.json) records the creative directions and selected copy.

All brands, products, venues, offers, prices and inclusions are fictional campaign examples. Replace them with verified campaign details, dates, terms and sender identification before sending. Background images are illustrative, not documentary proof. Keep unsubscribe substitution configured in your delivery platform. No fake testimonials, campaign performance statistics or guaranteed charity allocations are used.

The catalog retains its existing two-tag taxonomy: one broad industry and one purpose. Event names live in the template names, descriptions and this index. New asset URLs have not been published; the application’s pinned snapshot and hosted tenant libraries are unchanged. Browser previews do not certify every email client.
