THE LUNCH FORMULA

01

BUILD A BASE

 

Use bread, rice, potatoes or another favourite staple as a starting point.

02

ADD SOMETHING FILLING

 

Beans, eggs, fish, tofu or leftovers can help turn a snack plate into lunch.

03

MAKE IT TASTE GOOD

 

Add vegetables you like, a dressing and something crunchy. Adapt the plate to your appetite.
