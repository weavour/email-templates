# Begin Gently Copy

## Brief

Audience: people interested in yoga studio. Context: a warm seasonal email audience. Primary goal: book the new year practice. Offer: New Year studio practice · £18. Tone follows the fictional sender and campaign; all commercial claims are illustrative and need owner verification. No testimonials, statistics or real-world endorsements are invented.

## Selected copy

Subject: A gentler way to begin the year

Preheader: A welcoming New Year yoga session, with no experience needed.

Headline: Begin gently.

Introduction: You don’t need a new version of yourself to start the year. Join our New Year practice for simple movement, a slower breath and a little space.

Primary CTA: Book the New Year practice

Offer: New Year studio practice · £18

Practical detail: A 75-minute session with tea afterwards.

Section: A little room, just as you are.

### Arrive

Settle onto your mat and meet the teacher.

### Move

Follow a gentle sequence with options explained throughout.

### Stay

Finish with a quiet rest and an unhurried cup of tea.

Closing headline: Start where you are. That’s enough.

## Rationale

The subject identifies the actual seasonal opportunity. The headline gives Stillday its own voice; the introduction explains the offer in everyday language. Each section answers a concrete question about choosing, attending or using it. Both action buttons share the same specific destination. A 75-minute session with tea afterwards. supplies a practical decision point rather than unsupported proof or artificial urgency.
