# Cyber Monday copy — Lingua House

## Brief

Audience: people interested in online language courses. Context: a warm seasonal email audience. Primary goal: find my beginner course. Offer: 25% off selected beginner courses. Tone follows the fictional sender and campaign; all commercial claims are illustrative and need owner verification. No testimonials, statistics or real-world endorsements are invented.

## Selected copy

Subject: Cyber Monday: your next language course, 25% off

Preheader: Live beginner lessons, useful conversations and a clear first step.

Headline: More than knowing the words.

Introduction: Start speaking with a live course that gives you room to practise. Save 25% on selected beginner courses this Cyber Monday.

Primary CTA: Find my beginner course

Offer: 25% off selected beginner courses

Practical detail: Choose your language and course schedule online.

Section: A little practice, week by week.

### Start where you are

Read the level guide and choose a beginner group.

### Use real situations

Practise introductions, questions and everyday conversations.

### Keep speaking

Build on each lesson with short activities between classes.

Closing headline: Your first conversation starts with a hello.

## Rationale

The subject identifies the actual seasonal opportunity. The headline gives Lingua House its own voice; the introduction explains the offer in everyday language. Each section answers a concrete question about choosing, attending or using it. Both action buttons share the same specific destination. Choose your language and course schedule online. supplies a practical decision point rather than unsupported proof or artificial urgency.

## Unused alternatives

Headline B: Cyber Monday: your next language course, 25% off — A more literal seasonal message.

Headline C: Your first conversation starts with a hello. — A warmer benefit-led opening for an already familiar audience.

CTA B: Choose my beginner course — An alternative action verb with the same destination.

CTA C: Find my beginner course with Lingua House — A sender-specific version for a less familiar audience.

Only the selected copy ships in the email. No SEO metadata is required for this artifact. Replace course languages, schedules, inclusions and discount terms with the real programme.
