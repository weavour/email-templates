## Rationale

The subject identifies the actual seasonal opportunity. The headline gives Spring Common its own voice; the introduction explains the offer in everyday language. Each section answers a concrete question about choosing, attending or using it. Both action buttons share the same specific destination. Accompanying adults join free. supplies a practical decision point rather than unsupported proof or artificial urgency.
