# The Lantern Trail Copy

## Brief

Audience: people interested in outdoor family attraction. Context: a warm seasonal email audience. Primary goal: book our lantern-trail visit. Offer: Family trail tickets from £28. Tone follows the fictional sender and campaign; all commercial claims are illustrative and need owner verification. No testimonials, statistics or real-world endorsements are invented.

## Selected copy

Subject: The Halloween lantern trail is lighting up

Preheader: A family walk through pumpkins, stories and autumn woods.

Headline: Follow the lights. Find the fun.

Introduction: Pull on your boots for a Halloween walk through glowing pumpkins, woodland stories and a little after-dark adventure.

Primary CTA: Book our lantern-trail visit

Offer: Family trail tickets from £28

Practical detail: Choose your entry time when booking.

Section: Your guide to a brighter dark.

### Dress for the woods

Bring warm layers, comfortable boots and a sense of adventure.

### Follow the glow

Walk the marked route at your own pace and look out for story stops.

### Finish warm

Leave time for a hot drink at the end of the trail.

Closing headline: A little spooky. A lot to remember.

## Rationale

The subject identifies the actual seasonal opportunity. The headline gives Lantern Trail its own voice; the introduction explains the offer in everyday language. Each section answers a concrete question about choosing, attending or using it. Both action buttons share the same specific destination. Choose your entry time when booking. supplies a practical decision point rather than unsupported proof or artificial urgency.
