# A Smoother Sunday Ride Copy

## Brief

Audience: people interested in cycling shop. Context: a warm seasonal email audience. Primary goal: choose a bike-service gift voucher. Offer: Service gift vouchers from £45. Tone follows the fictional sender and campaign; all commercial claims are illustrative and need owner verification. No testimonials, statistics or real-world endorsements are invented.

## Selected copy

Subject: A Father’s Day gift for his next ride

Preheader: A service voucher he can book when it suits him.

Headline: Give him a smoother Sunday ride.

Introduction: A bike-service voucher is a useful kind of Father’s Day gift. He picks the appointment; we help get his bike ready for the road.

Primary CTA: Choose a bike-service gift voucher

Offer: Service gift vouchers from £45

Practical detail: Select an amount and add your message.

Section: A gift with the practical part included.

### Choose

Pick the voucher value that fits your gift.

### Give

Add a note and send it to yourself or directly to him.

### Book

He contacts the workshop to choose a suitable service appointment.

Closing headline: For wherever the next ride takes him.

## Rationale

The subject identifies the actual seasonal opportunity. The headline gives Spoke & Sons its own voice; the introduction explains the offer in everyday language. Each section answers a concrete question about choosing, attending or using it. Both action buttons share the same specific destination. Select an amount and add your message. supplies a practical decision point rather than unsupported proof or artificial urgency.
