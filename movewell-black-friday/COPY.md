# Make Room for Movement Copy

## Brief

Audience: people interested in pilates studio. Context: a warm seasonal email audience. Primary goal: choose my class pack. Offer: Buy 10 classes. Get 2 extra.. Tone follows the fictional sender and campaign; all commercial claims are illustrative and need owner verification. No testimonials, statistics or real-world endorsements are invented.

## Selected copy

Subject: Your Black Friday class pack: ten classes, plus two

Preheader: A flexible way to make movement part of your week.

Headline: Make room for movement.

Introduction: Start with a class pack that fits your week. Our Black Friday offer adds two classes when you buy a ten-class pass.

Primary CTA: Choose my class pack

Offer: Buy 10 classes. Get 2 extra.

Practical detail: Valid on the selected studio class pass.

Section: Find a rhythm you can keep.

### Pick your classes

Browse the timetable for a pace and time that suit you.

### Use your pass

Book each class through your account as you go.

### Start comfortably

Check the class descriptions and ask us where to begin.

Closing headline: One class is a good place to start.

## Rationale

The subject identifies the actual seasonal opportunity. The headline gives Movewell its own voice; the introduction explains the offer in everyday language. Each section answers a concrete question about choosing, attending or using it. Both action buttons share the same specific destination. Valid on the selected studio class pass. supplies a practical decision point rather than unsupported proof or artificial urgency.
