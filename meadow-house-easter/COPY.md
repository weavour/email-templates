## Rationale

The subject identifies the actual seasonal opportunity. The headline gives Meadow House its own voice; the introduction explains the offer in everyday language. Each section answers a concrete question about choosing, attending or using it. Both action buttons share the same specific destination. Based on two guests sharing, with breakfast. supplies a practical decision point rather than unsupported proof or artificial urgency.
