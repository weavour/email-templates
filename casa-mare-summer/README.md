# A slower shade of summer

An image-rich promotion for Casa Mare, a fictional Mediterranean boutique hotel. Inspired by the campaign variety and image-led storytelling in [Unlayer’s gallery](https://unlayer.com/templates), with original composition, copy and imagery.

## Design

Ocean blue #174aa0, warm limestone #fbf7ee, deep sea ink #173c51, pale coastal gray #e9eff0 and a restrained lemon #f0d77c booking action. Georgia carries the editorial headlines; Arial keeps body copy and actions email-safe. The 600px canvas opens with a quiet masthead and a full-bleed villa photograph, then an editorial invitation. A portrait suite and short story, a reversed breakfast pairing, a second wide cove image and a blue booking panel create distinct reading rhythms. Four photographs carry atmosphere; every headline, description and call to action remains a native editable block. Paired columns remain side by side at narrow widths.

## Image provenance

All four artworks were newly generated for this template with OpenAI’s built-in image-generation tool. No stock, competitor artwork or reused catalog imagery is included. Originals were converted to email-friendly JPEGs with a maximum 1200px edge. Each JPEG embeds its exact generation prompt; the SHA-256 filename covers the final bytes, including metadata. No campaign text is baked into the pictures. Keep URLs immutable after publication.
