# Wildform email copy

## Brief and assumptions

One primary action: book a place at a beginner botanical sun-print workshop. Audience: curious beginners choosing a hands-on creative activity. Email context: a warm studio mailing list. Tone: welcoming, practical and quietly playful. The studio, event, inclusions and price are fictional template examples, not researched customer facts. No testimonials, outcome statistics, scarcity or real-world endorsements are invented.

## Selected email copy

Subject: Make your first botanical sun print

Preheader: Two hours, two prints and everything you need to begin.

Headline: Make art with a little sunshine.

Introduction: Turn leaves and sunlight into your first botanical blue print. Join our beginner-friendly cyanotype workshop.

Primary CTA, repeated with the same destination: Book the sun-print workshop

Workshop section: A little light. A print to keep.

Cyanotype is a simple way to make deep-blue prints with real plants. Learn the process, make two prints, and leave with the skills to try it again.

10:00 — Find your shape. Start with a short demo. Choose your leaves and arrange your first print.

10:30 — Let the light in. We’ll guide you through exposing the paper and rinsing your design.

11:15 — Make it your own. Try a second composition. Compare your prints over a cup of tea while they dry.

Friction reducer: Paper, plants, aprons and tea are included. Just bring yourself. No drawing skills needed.

Booking headline: Leave with something you made.

Practical details: Saturday 22 May 2027 · 10:00–12:00 · £48, all materials · The Glasshouse Studio, 18 Green Lane.

CTA support: Book one place. Make two prints.

Brand close: Made by nature. Finished by you.

## Why these choices

- The subject names the concrete activity, while the preheader adds duration, output and inclusions.
- The headline gives the creative promise a memorable voice; the next sentence explains the workshop without requiring prior knowledge of cyanotype.
- Each scheduled section answers what happens next, replacing abstract benefit claims with an understandable process.
- Materials and drawing confidence address two common beginner concerns without guarantees or pressure.
- Both action buttons use the same specific booking label and destination. The supporting promise makes the illustrative offer easy to understand.

## Unused alternatives

Headline B: Turn a few leaves into your first sun print. — More literal; useful for an unfamiliar audience.

Headline C: Your next Saturday, in Prussian blue. — More editorial; useful for an existing studio audience.

CTA B: Book my botanical print workshop — Personal wording with a clear commitment.

CTA C: Reserve a sun-print place — Shorter wording for narrow placements.
