# Start with one note

A public editable email template for fictional Patch, created using the email-design skill. The [visual study](design/concept.png) and [design intent](design/intent.md) were created before inspecting the native implementation schema.

## Design

A light steel-paper technical manual with graphite live type, restrained olive annotations, the physical hardware photograph near the opening, a headline below it, a compact horizontal signal path and a ruled specification and ordering ledger. The photograph supplies the vivid lime accent. The imagery and information rhythm belong to an instrument maker rather than a software newsletter.

Typography: Arial/Helvetica/sans-serif for display and body text; Courier New/Courier/monospace for model and engineering annotations. No external web fonts. All essential wording, product details, prices, actions and layout remain native editable Weavour blocks. Artwork contains no lettering or essential campaign details. JSON is the source and HTML is its actual Weavour renderer export.

Implementation interpretation: The initial dark patch-sheet concept was revised after comparing the ten-template set for variety. The final light manual study precedes the revised native implementation. Model notation is modest, the photograph moves before the headline, and the signal path is horizontal native columns. Physical panel labels are deliberately absent from the original photograph, keeping product details in live text. No entire email image or HTML block is used.

### Actual artwork prompt

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: landscape macro editorial hardware photograph for a modular music instrument maker's editable email, not a mockup. An unbranded compact real analogue-style synthesizer on a midnight-blue workbench. Close oblique crop across matte charcoal metal faceplate, four beautifully knurled silver aluminium control knobs, a few empty small audio sockets and two vivid acid-chartreuse patch cables looping into sockets, one tiny warm amber indicator light. Physical precision, real brushed metal and cable rubber textures, low directional studio light, crisp product photography, restrained blue-black shadows. The pictured panel is entirely unlabelled: no printed words, numbers, scales, diagrams, logos or markings. Landscape 3:2 framing, tactile close-up and cables fully readable as cables, no person or hands, no UI or screen. No typography, watermark, border or lettering.

### Actual visual study prompt

> Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: revised full-email visual design study for PATCH, fictional maker of modular music hardware. One straight-on narrow technical instrument manual, 600px wide by about1100px tall, no device or browser. The whole email is LIGHT steel-paper #e6e9e5 with dark graphite #20282c typography and restrained olive #4d650a annotations. Avoid a dark background or billboard treatment.
> A small monospaced PATCH masthead and tiny 'INSTRUMENT SHEET / 01', a fine rule, then a dominant wide macro photograph of unbranded matte charcoal synthesizer hardware with precision silver knobs and two acid-lime patch cables. Below the photograph place the bold charcoal headline 'Start with one note.' in a practical heavy Arial-like sans serif, with a short sentence about hands-on sound.
> Then a compact HORIZONTAL signal path, three native-looking labels connected by arrows across one row: 'TONE → SHAPE → OUT'. Under each, one concise phrase: 'Find a frequency', 'Move the filter', 'Hear the change'. This is a small engineering notation, no cards, no vertical numbered programme, no oversized numeric headline.
> Continue with a quiet ruled specification ledger on the light paper, two columns with labels 'FORMAT', 'CONTROLS', 'IN THE BOX', and short descriptions about a compact desktop instrument, knobs and patch points, and power supply with two cables. Finish like a manual's ordering panel: modest 'UNIT 01', large dark £249, dark rectangular 'Explore Unit 01' button aligned alongside, small PATCH footer and Unsubscribe. The image supplies a vivid lime accent without flooding the layout with it. Disciplined, tactile, readable, genuinely manual-like. No rounded cards, gradients, SaaS screens, generic hero/three-card stack, decorative circuitry or invented certification logos.
