# Start with one note

Patch is a fictional modular music hardware studio launching Unit 01, a compact hands-on instrument. The action is to explore the instrument; £249 and all product details are sample content.

## Final visual direction

A light steel-paper technical instrument manual, not a dark billboard. Put a small model index in the masthead, then the physical instrument photograph near the opening. The headline follows the photograph. A compact horizontal native signal path reads TONE → SHAPE → OUT, followed by a short ruled specification ledger and a manual-like ordering close.

Palette: steel paper #e6e9e5, graphite #20282c, olive #4d650a and supporting slate #46525a. The original photograph supplies the vivid lime patch-cable accent. Use heavy system sans-serif for the live title and practical monospace annotations. Keep ample open paper and avoid numbered vertical process rows.

## Imagery and copy

Use the original unlabelled synthesizer macro photograph with physical silver knobs, charcoal metal, lime cables and amber indicator. Keep all actual model details, specifications and actions as native editable text. Headline: Start with one note. Supporting copy: Hands-on sound for curious minds. Unit 01 is a compact instrument for following an idea with your fingers.

The three signal descriptions are Find a frequency, Move the filter and Hear the change. Specifications are concise and clearly fictional. Finish with Unit 01, £249 and Explore Unit 01.
