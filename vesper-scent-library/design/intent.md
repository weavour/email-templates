# Wear the weather

Vesper is a fictional independent perfumery launching Petrichor, a green mineral perfume. Make an ivory-and-aubergine scent editorial: a balanced but asymmetric opening spread, a portrait bottle photograph, live large serif type, a short horizontal note index, and a restrained price/booklet close. It should feel like a perfume blotter crossed with a weather notebook, not a beauty product card grid. Headline: Wear the weather. Supporting words: Before the rain. After the rush. Notes are sensory descriptions, not weather forecasts. Keep the campaign action to explore the 30ml fragrance, with a fictional £64 offer. Mobile preserves the text/art pair using short lines and readable type. Production words remain native.

Design first: create a visual study before inspecting the native block schema. Keep study images separate from production assets.

## Implementation and visual review

The concept’s oversized opening typography is reduced to fit the text/photo spread at 320px; the portrait image retains its full framing and the title remains live text. Gradients and image-baked lettering from the study are not used. Desktop and mobile renders were compared with the study.
