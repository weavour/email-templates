# Let the day come to rest

An original, native-editable invitation for the fictional Lune wellbeing studio. The campaign is an illustrative example; the sender, facilitator, workshop, price and schedule are fictional.

## Design

A bedside letter in dusk blue (#23344e), parchment (#f7f3e9) and lavender (#e6def0). Large Georgia lettering establishes a quiet invitation; the letter gives way to a practical, time-led sequence of three evening rituals. The restrained online-workshop details and a single reservation action close the composition. Arial body text, generous spacing and responsive native columns keep the reading order comfortable on mobile.

Every heading, paragraph, time, layout and action remains editable. This template intentionally uses no imagery: its typographic letter and time-led ritual sequence are the design, without irrelevant stock photography or decorative fake artwork.

## Personalization

Replace the Lune brand, facilitator, workshop details, date, price, recording policy and example.com link before sending. The example event is Wednesday 17 November 2027, 19:30–20:45 GMT. Set your sender details through your delivery platform and preserve/substitute {{unsubscribe_url}}. The copy makes no medical or treatment claims.
