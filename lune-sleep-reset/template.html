A gentler evening starts with a little space.

Dear night owl,

 

Some evenings, the day follows us to bed. The unfinished list. One more message. The feeling that we should still be doing something.

 

Join us for an unhurried workshop on making a little room between a busy day and bedtime. We’ll explore small rituals you can adapt to your own evening, without turning rest into another task.

 

Until then, here’s a quiet place to begin.

 

With care, Mara & the Lune team

An evening, with less hurry.

 

Three invitations for the hour before bed. Take what feels useful; leave the rest.

9:00

Close the loop.

 

Write tomorrow’s first task on paper. Let the list wait there until morning.

9:20

Soften the room.

 

Lower the lights. Set your phone aside. Choose a book, a warm drink or a few quiet minutes.

9:40

Make a little room.

 

Sit comfortably and notice your breath. There’s nothing to achieve and no perfect way to finish.
