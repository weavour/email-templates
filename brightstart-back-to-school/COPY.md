# A Clearer School Year Copy

## Brief

Audience: people interested in tutoring. Context: a warm seasonal email audience. Primary goal: book an introductory tutoring session. Offer: Introductory tutoring session · £20. Tone follows the fictional sender and campaign; all commercial claims are illustrative and need owner verification. No testimonials, statistics or real-world endorsements are invented.

## Selected copy

Subject: A clearer first step for the new school year

Preheader: Meet a tutor, talk through priorities and plan the next step.

Headline: Start the term with a clearer plan.

Introduction: A new school year can bring a lot of questions. Our introductory tutoring session helps you work out where to begin and what support could look like.

Primary CTA: Book an introductory tutoring session

Offer: Introductory tutoring session · £20

Practical detail: A first conversation for the student and parent or carer.

Section: Start with a useful conversation.

### Share

Tell us about the subject, school stage and current concerns.

### Explore

Meet a tutor and talk through a few example tasks.

### Plan

Discuss a possible approach without committing to a package.

Closing headline: A little clarity before the term gets busy.

## Rationale

The subject identifies the actual seasonal opportunity. The headline gives Brightstart its own voice; the introduction explains the offer in everyday language. Each section answers a concrete question about choosing, attending or using it. Both action buttons share the same specific destination. A first conversation for the student and parent or carer. supplies a practical decision point rather than unsupported proof or artificial urgency.
