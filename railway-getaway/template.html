WEEKEND

 

Leave Friday. Come back different.

 

A city break, a coastal pause or a night in the hills. Let the train do the moving.

01 / THE HARBOUR WEEKEND

 

Two nights near the water, a walkable old town and a first-class bakery.

02 / THE HILLSIDE STAY

 

A small inn, a waymarked loop and a good dinner at the end of it.

03 / THE CITY RESET

 

Galleries, cafés and a hotel close enough to the station to leave the taxi out.
