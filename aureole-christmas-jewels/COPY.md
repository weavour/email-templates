# Gifts Close to Heart Copy

## Brief

Audience: people interested in independent jewelry. Context: a warm seasonal email audience. Primary goal: find their christmas piece. Offer: Christmas gift wrapping included. Tone follows the fictional sender and campaign; all commercial claims are illustrative and need owner verification. No testimonials, statistics or real-world endorsements are invented.

## Selected copy

Subject: A Christmas gift that stays close

Preheader: Personal jewelry, gift wrapping and a note in your words.

Headline: For the one you know by heart.

Introduction: A small gift can hold a lot. Find a piece they’ll reach for long after the wrapping is gone.

Primary CTA: Find their Christmas piece

Offer: Christmas gift wrapping included

Practical detail: Choose a personal note at checkout.

Section: A piece for their everyday.

### The Quiet Pendant

A simple gold-vermeil curve on a fine adjustable chain. From £78.

### The Everyday Hoops

Small, softly rounded hoops made to go with almost everything. From £54.

Closing headline: Give something that feels like them.

## Rationale

The subject identifies the actual seasonal opportunity. The headline gives Auréole its own voice; the introduction explains the offer in everyday language. Each section answers a concrete question about choosing, attending or using it. Both action buttons share the same specific destination. Choose a personal note at checkout. supplies a practical decision point rather than unsupported proof or artificial urgency.

## Unused alternatives

Headline B: A Christmas gift that stays close — A more literal seasonal message.

Headline C: Give something that feels like them. — A warmer benefit-led opening for an already familiar audience.

CTA B: Choose their Christmas piece — An alternative action verb with the same destination.

CTA C: Find their Christmas piece with Auréole — A sender-specific version for a less familiar audience.

Only the selected copy ships in the email. No SEO metadata is required for this artifact. Confirm metal, sizing, dispatch dates and gift-service details on the product page.
