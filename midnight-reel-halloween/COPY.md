# The Midnight Double Bill Copy

## Brief

Audience: people interested in independent cinema. Context: a warm seasonal email audience. Primary goal: book the halloween double bill. Offer: Two films, one ticket · £18. Tone follows the fictional sender and campaign; all commercial claims are illustrative and need owner verification. No testimonials, statistics or real-world endorsements are invented.

## Selected copy

Subject: Your Halloween double bill is here

Preheader: Two fictional fright-night features, one cinema ticket.

Headline: Two films. One very good reason to stay up.

Introduction: Our Halloween double bill pairs slow-building suspense with a late-night creature feature. Bring a friend who’ll share the popcorn.

Primary CTA: Book the Halloween double bill

Offer: Two films, one ticket · £18

Practical detail: A short interval separates the screenings.

Section: The night’s programme.

### First screening

The House Beyond the Pines — a slow-burn tale of an unwelcome return.

### At the interval

Stretch your legs, refill the popcorn and debate the ending.

### Second screening

Something in the Lake — a midnight creature story with a mischievous streak.

Closing headline: Stay for the second scream.

## Rationale

The subject identifies the actual seasonal opportunity. The headline gives Midnight Reel its own voice; the introduction explains the offer in everyday language. Each section answers a concrete question about choosing, attending or using it. Both action buttons share the same specific destination. A short interval separates the screenings. supplies a practical decision point rather than unsupported proof or artificial urgency.
