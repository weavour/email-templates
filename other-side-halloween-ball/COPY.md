# Be Someone Else Copy

## Brief

Audience: adults looking for a creative Halloween night out. Context: a warm event or arts-venue mailing list. One primary action: book a Halloween ticket. Voice: bold, theatrical and playful, with practical event information after the invitation. All event details are fictional template examples.

## Selected copy

Subject: Halloween, on the Other Side

Preheader: 31 October. Costumes, curious performances and late-night DJs.

Headline: BE SOMEONE ELSE.

Event: THE HALLOWEEN MASQUERADE

Introduction: Costumes, late-night DJs and curious performances. Step inside our Halloween masquerade and give the everyday you the night off.

Primary CTA: Book my Halloween ticket

Programme heading: WHAT’S ON THE OTHER SIDE.

20:00 — MAKE AN ENTRANCE. Find your people, finish your mask and get your first look around.

21:00 — FOLLOW THE STRANGE. Pop-up performances and unexpected characters make the room part of the show.

22:00 — STAY FOR THE LAST TRACK. Our DJs take the floor from there. You can keep your secret identity.

Friction reducer: Costumes encouraged. We’ll have masks if you need one.

Details: The Old Observatory · Saturday 31 October 2026 · 20:00–01:00 · £28, entry and mask bar · 18+. Ends 01:00 Sunday 1 November.

Closing headline: LEAVE NORMAL AT THE DOOR.

CTA support: One ticket. A different version of you.

Brand close: A Halloween night out of the ordinary.

## Why it works

The short headline sells the social permission of a masquerade; the next lines immediately explain the event. The programme turns an atmospheric promise into a concrete evening. A mask option reduces costume anxiety. Date, venue, age and price support an informed booking decision. Both buttons use the same specific action and destination. There is no invented scarcity, attendance figure or testimonial.

## Unused alternatives

Headline B: YOUR ALTER EGO HAS PLANS. — More playful; the event subtitle supplies the context.

Headline C: HALLOWEEN LIVES ON THE OTHER SIDE. — More literal, suitable for an unfamiliar audience.

CTA B: Reserve my masquerade ticket — Names the event format directly.

CTA C: Join the Other Side this Halloween — A more branded invitation.
