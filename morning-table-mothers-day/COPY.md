# Breakfast for Mum Copy

## Brief

Audience: people interested in brunch café. Context: a warm seasonal email audience. Primary goal: reserve our mother’s day brunch. Offer: Mother’s Day brunch · £24 per person. Tone follows the fictional sender and campaign; all commercial claims are illustrative and need owner verification. No testimonials, statistics or real-world endorsements are invented.

## Selected copy

Subject: A Mother’s Day table, with breakfast taken care of

Preheader: Choose your brunch time and bring the good company.

Headline: Let someone else do the breakfast.

Introduction: Bring Mum to the table and we’ll take care of the rest. Our Mother’s Day brunch is made for long conversations and a second cup.

Primary CTA: Reserve our Mother’s Day brunch

Offer: Mother’s Day brunch · £24 per person

Practical detail: Includes a main dish and a hot drink.

Section: A very good start to the day.

### Something savoury

Eggs on sourdough, with seasonal greens and your choice of sides.

### Something sweet

French toast, bright berries and a little maple syrup.

### Something to linger over

Coffee, tea and no reason to rush the conversation.

Closing headline: Her morning. Your treat.

## Rationale

The subject identifies the actual seasonal opportunity. The headline gives The Morning Table its own voice; the introduction explains the offer in everyday language. Each section answers a concrete question about choosing, attending or using it. Both action buttons share the same specific destination. Includes a main dish and a hot drink. supplies a practical decision point rather than unsupported proof or artificial urgency.
