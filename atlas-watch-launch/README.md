# Hours worth keeping

A text-led watch launch for the fictional Atlas brand. The layout reads as a product dossier: a restrained masthead, an asymmetric introduction with an oversized model number on rust, a four-part specification sheet, and a closing note from the design desk. This composition is original; Unlayer's template gallery supplied the broad inspiration for a reusable, campaign-specific email.

## Design

Warm ivory `#f5f0e7`, charcoal `#272822`, muted ink `#625f55`, rust `#98462f` and stone `#e8e1d4`. Email-safe Georgia provides the editorial display voice; Arial carries specifications and body copy. All authored content uses native editable blocks. Columns remain paired on narrow screens with readable type, and both calls to action remain real links.

## Personalization

The sender, watch, specifications and quotation are fictional sample campaign content, not claims about a real product. Replace them, the example.com destinations and sender details before sending. Keep `{{unsubscribe_url}}` intact and substitute it through your delivery system.

## Image provenance

No images are used. The design intentionally relies on typography, proportion, color fields and native layout blocks rather than unrelated product photography. No licensed or generated artwork is required.

`template.json` is the editable source. `template.html` and desktop/mobile screenshots are generated by the sibling application's actual Weavour exporter.
