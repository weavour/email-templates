# The Long Way Outside Copy

## Brief

Audience: people interested in outdoor equipment. Context: a warm seasonal email audience. Primary goal: explore the trail-gear offers. Offer: Up to 30% off selected gear. Tone follows the fictional sender and campaign; all commercial claims are illustrative and need owner verification. No testimonials, statistics or real-world endorsements are invented.

## Selected copy

Subject: The Ridgeway Black Friday trail edit

Preheader: Selected outdoor kit, with savings up to 30%.

Headline: Take the long way. Spend a little less.

Introduction: Black Friday is a good time to sort the kit for your next walk. Save up to 30% on selected packs, layers and trail essentials.

Primary CTA: Explore the trail-gear offers

Offer: Up to 30% off selected gear

Practical detail: Savings vary by item and are shown online.

Section: Pack for the walk you want.

### Carry comfortably

Choose a pack with the space and fit for your route.

### Layer for the forecast

Look for practical layers that work together.

### Check the small things

Make room for water, route information and a spare dry layer.

Closing headline: The next good day starts outside.

## Rationale

The subject identifies the actual seasonal opportunity. The headline gives Ridgeway its own voice; the introduction explains the offer in everyday language. Each section answers a concrete question about choosing, attending or using it. Both action buttons share the same specific destination. Savings vary by item and are shown online. supplies a practical decision point rather than unsupported proof or artificial urgency.
