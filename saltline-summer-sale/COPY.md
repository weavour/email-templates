# More Sea Days Copy

## Brief

Audience: people interested in swimwear. Context: a warm seasonal email audience. Primary goal: shop the summer swimwear sale. Offer: 25% off selected swimwear. Tone follows the fictional sender and campaign; all commercial claims are illustrative and need owner verification. No testimonials, statistics or real-world endorsements are invented.

## Selected copy

Subject: The Saltline summer sale: selected swimwear, 25% off

Preheader: Find your next swim-day favourite in the summer edit.

Headline: More sea days. A little less to spend.

Introduction: Our summer sale takes 25% off selected swimwear. Find the shape and colour you’ll pack for a swim, a weekend or a long afternoon by the water.

Primary CTA: Shop the summer swimwear sale

Offer: 25% off selected swimwear

Practical detail: Check size and colour availability on each product page.

Section: Make room in the beach bag.

### The one-piece

A clean, simple silhouette for long days by the water.

### The mix-and-match set

Choose the top and bottom sizes that work for you.

Closing headline: Your next dip is a good enough reason.

## Rationale

The subject identifies the actual seasonal opportunity. The headline gives Saltline its own voice; the introduction explains the offer in everyday language. Each section answers a concrete question about choosing, attending or using it. Both action buttons share the same specific destination. Check size and colour availability on each product page. supplies a practical decision point rather than unsupported proof or artificial urgency.
