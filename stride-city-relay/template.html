CITY RELAY 18 APRIL 2027

Your city. Your team. Your pace.

 

One baton. Twenty kilometres. A finish line you cross together.

The best part is who you run with.

 

Bring your usual running crew, your workmates or three friends who are up for something new. Stride City Relay is an untimed morning on the riverside, with a five-kilometre loop for every teammate.

THE DISTANCE

 

5 km each

 

Four laps. Four runners. A flat riverside loop.

THE START

 

09:00

 

Sunday 18 April Riverside Exchange

Know your lap.

 
 

Start at Riverside Exchange

Follow the promenade past the market, turn at Mill Bridge and return along the water. Every runner follows the same signed loop.

 

Find your team in the changeover zone

Marshals guide each handover. Once your final runner returns, regroup for a finish-line photo and coffee by the river.

 

All paces welcome

Run, jog or mix in a walk. This is a shared morning out, with no chip times or podium pressure.

Four places. One good Sunday.

 

£48 per team of four. Your entry includes race bibs, a shared baton and water at the exchange. Bring your own refillable bottle.

 
Register your team
 

Team check-in opens at 08:15. Registration closes 11 April or when places fill.
