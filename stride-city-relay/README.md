# Four Friends One City

A native editable invitation for a fictional Stride City Relay. The composition starts as a lime race bib: the 4 × 5 distance anchors an asymmetric opening, followed by a personal invitation, paired distance/start facts, a route briefing and an ultramarine registration panel.

## Design

Ultramarine #163bc4, lime #d8f565, deep ink #132148, pale blue #edf1ff and white. Email-safe Arial typography, with a 60px unbroken distance figure, compact athletic headlines and 16px body copy. The layout uses native sections, paired columns that remain side-by-side on mobile, headings, paragraphs, dividers, spacers, a button and unsubscribe block. No content is baked into images.

## Personalize before sending
